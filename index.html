Why Clayton Miles

Built around better hiring.

Recruitment should reduce pressure, not add to it. Our UK recruitment service gives employers a focused, straightforward way to find qualified permanent and temporary staff across our specialist sectors.

Rigorously screened candidates

Every candidate is assessed against your job description, required experience, qualifications, availability and preferences before introduction. We also discuss their motivation and suitability, giving you a focused shortlist without the time spent reviewing unsuitable applications.

Free replacement guarantee

If your new hire leaves during the replacement period agreed in your recruitment terms, we will carry out a replacement search at no additional recruitment fee, subject to those terms. This gives you added reassurance when making an important hiring decision.

Pay after a successful placement

Our contingency recruitment service has no upfront search fee. We define your candidate profile, search the market and introduce suitable professionals before a fee is due. You pay only after you successfully hire a candidate we introduce.
