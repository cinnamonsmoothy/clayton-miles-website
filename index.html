Everything you need to know.

Straight answers about working with Clayton Miles.

How quickly can you send candidate CVs?

For many roles, we can send suitable CVs within 24–48 hours. Timing can vary depending on the role, location and requirements.

Which industries do you recruit for?

We recruit across health and social care, housing, hospitality, logistics and transport, automotive, engineering, education, admin and HR, finance and legal.

When do we pay for your service?

There is no upfront recruitment fee. Payment follows once you have finalised a successful placement.

What happens if a candidate leaves?

We offer a free replacement if a candidate leaves within the agreed rebate period.

How do we start a search?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or email the team with the role, location and requirements. We will get back to you to discuss the brief.
