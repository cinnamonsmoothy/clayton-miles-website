Adult Social Care Recruitment

Dependable people for essential care services.

The right appointment helps protect continuity, strengthen the team and give the people using a service consistent support.

Clayton Miles is a UK social care recruitment agency supporting residential homes, nursing homes, domiciliary care, supported living and complex-care providers. We recruit Registered Managers, Care Home Managers, Care Assistants, Support Workers, Care Coordinators and operational professionals for permanent and temporary positions.

Every service operates differently, even when the job titles look familiar. We discuss the people supported, the care model, team structure, rota, location and day-to-day responsibilities before identifying candidates. This helps us understand which experience is essential and where a candidate's skills may transfer successfully from another setting.

Our care home recruitment service covers leadership and frontline appointments. A Registered Manager or Home Manager search may involve team development, occupancy, quality, budgets and relationships with families and professionals. Care Assistant and Support Worker recruitment places greater emphasis on practical experience, communication, availability and the ability to provide consistent support.

Temporary care staffing can help providers respond to absence, vacancies and changing demand, while permanent recruitment supports continuity and long-term team development. We confirm shifts, travel, availability and the role-specific evidence agreed with you before presenting a candidate.

Working with a specialist care staffing agency gives managers a focused route to people who may not be reached through a general advert. Whether you need one experienced manager, several support workers or continuing recruitment help across multiple services, we shape the search around the realities of your organisation.

Social Care FAQ

What care providers ask us.

Answers about roles, temporary staffing, turnaround, coverage and recruitment fees.

Which social care roles do you recruit?

We recruit registered managers, home managers, care assistants, support workers, care coordinators and other adult social care professionals.

Do you supply temporary and permanent care staff?

Yes. We support permanent appointments, planned temporary cover and urgent staffing requirements subject to the role, location and candidate availability.

Which care providers do you support?

We support care homes, nursing homes, domiciliary care, supported living and complex-care services, from independent providers to multi-site groups.

How do you check candidates?

We assess experience, employment history, availability and agreed role-specific evidence. Employers retain responsibility for final suitability and compliance decisions.

Can you help with urgent or short-notice care cover?

Yes. We can prioritise urgent care staffing requirements where suitable candidates are available. Turnaround depends on the service, shift, location, rate and experience required.

How quickly can you find social care staff?

We aim to provide suitable candidates within 24–48 hours for many requirements. Management searches, difficult shifts and some locations may take longer, but we will provide a realistic timescale after discussing the brief.

How much does social care recruitment cost?

Permanent recruitment is offered on a contingency basis with no upfront search fee. Temporary rates depend on the role, shift, location, notice period and assignment requirements. Call us for a clear quote. We are transparent with pricing and will not waste your time.

Which areas of the UK do you cover?

We recruit nationwide and can discuss social care requirements across London, the Midlands, North West, Yorkshire, North East, South East, South West, Wales and Scotland. Candidate availability varies by service and shift pattern.

Can you recruit registered and care home managers?

Yes. We can run focused searches for Registered Managers, Care Home Managers, Nursing Home Managers and deputies, including confidential appointments where discretion is important.
