Boutique Art & Gallery Recruitment

The people behind the art make all the difference.

A successful appointment in the art market is rarely defined by a job title alone. Employers need people who understand the work, communicate with confidence and represent their organisation with the right degree of knowledge, care and discretion.

Clayton Miles is a UK art recruitment agency supporting employers across London and the wider UK. Our art gallery recruitment service is designed for commercial galleries, fine art businesses, auction houses, museums, art advisories and cultural organisations looking for a thoughtful, personal approach to hiring.

Every search starts with the context behind the position. When recruiting an Art Consultant, that may include collector relationships, fine art sales experience, artist knowledge, languages, an established network and the ability to advise clients with confidence. For Gallery Assistants and Gallery Managers, the detail may involve exhibitions, artist liaison, inventory, loans, shipping, installation schedules, art-fair logistics and day-to-day gallery operations. Depending on the brief, we can also discuss experience with Artlogic or other CRM and collections-management platforms, cataloguing, provenance, client due diligence, anti-money-laundering procedures and sanctions awareness.

Specialist arts recruitment also requires an understanding of how differently organisations operate. A founder-led gallery, international auction house, public museum and private advisory may use similar titles while expecting very different levels of autonomy, commercial awareness and stakeholder management. We clarify those distinctions before presenting candidates.

The hiring challenge

Specialist appointments require more than a familiar title.

The strongest candidate must understand the context of the organisation as well as the responsibilities of the position.

Experience can be highly specific

Primary and secondary markets, contemporary and historical works, private clients, public collections and international fairs each call for different knowledge. A credible search must distinguish genuinely relevant experience from a broadly similar CV.

Discretion protects relationships

The art market is small and interconnected. Senior moves, succession plans and confidential replacements need careful communication so employers can explore the market without creating unnecessary speculation.

Representation matters

Many employees become a visible extension of the gallery, auction house, museum or advisory. Judgement, presentation, cultural awareness and the ability to build trust can matter as much as technical competence.

Art Recruitment FAQ

What art-market employers ask us.

Every search is assessed against the specific brief. We review relevant art-market experience, responsibilities, commercial or technical knowledge, systems exposure, communication style, motivation, availability and working preferences, alongside right-to-work evidence and references where agreed.

Yes. We support art-market employers in London and across the wider UK. The search strategy is shaped around the location, working pattern, seniority and specialist knowledge required.

Can you manage a confidential art-market search?

Timing depends on the seniority and specificity of the brief. We will agree a realistic search plan at the outset and prioritise urgent temporary requirements where suitable people are available, without compromising the quality of the shortlist.

Yes. The art market includes many roles with distinctive combinations of subject knowledge, client relationships, sales ability, collections experience and operational expertise. We begin by defining what is essential and then shape a focused search around it.
