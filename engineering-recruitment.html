Technical & Engineering Recruitment

Technical people who keep operations moving.

Engineering appointments depend on the right combination of discipline, equipment knowledge, environment and hands-on responsibility.

Clayton Miles is a UK engineering recruitment agency supporting manufacturing, industrial and technical employers. We recruit maintenance, mechanical, electrical, production, quality, process, automation and project engineering professionals for permanent, fixed-term and contract requirements.

Engineering titles can conceal important differences. A Maintenance Engineer in a high-volume manufacturing site may work with very different machinery, controls and priorities from a Field Service Engineer or Project Engineer. We define the site, systems, shift pattern, technical scope and operational objectives before searching.

Our manufacturing recruitment work considers the equipment and processes candidates have supported, their balance of electrical and mechanical experience, fault-finding responsibilities and exposure to planned or reactive maintenance. For production, quality and process appointments, we explore the improvements, standards and measurable outcomes behind the CV.

Practical details can determine whether a technically strong candidate is genuinely suitable. We discuss shifts, call-outs, travel, site location, salary, contract duration and start date early. Where qualifications, tickets or other role-specific evidence are required, these are agreed as part of the brief and checked as appropriate before introduction.

Using a specialist engineering staffing agency helps employers reach professionals who may not be actively responding to general job adverts. Whether you are replacing one critical engineer, adding capability to a project team or recruiting across several sites, we provide a targeted search built around the technical problem you need the new hire to solve.

Engineering FAQ

What engineering employers ask us.

Answers about disciplines, contracts, turnaround, coverage and recruitment fees.

Which engineering roles do you recruit?

We recruit maintenance, mechanical, electrical, production, quality, process, project and automation engineering professionals.

Do you recruit permanent and contract engineers?

Yes. We support permanent, temporary, fixed-term and contract engineering requirements.

Which industries do you cover?

We support manufacturers, industrial operators, engineering services and other technical employers.

How do you assess engineering candidates?

We assess candidates against the technical brief, including discipline, equipment, systems, site environment and responsibilities, together with practical details such as shifts, travel and availability.

Can you help with urgent engineering cover?

Yes. We can prioritise urgent temporary and contract requirements where suitable engineers are available. Timing depends on the discipline, location, shift and specialist skills required.

How quickly can you find engineering staff?

We aim to provide suitable candidates within 24–48 hours for many requirements. Highly specialised, senior or location-specific searches may take longer, but we will provide a realistic timescale after taking the brief.

How much does engineering recruitment cost?

Permanent recruitment is offered on a contingency basis with no upfront search fee. Temporary and contract rates depend on the role, shift, location, notice period and assignment requirements. Call us for a clear quote. We are transparent with pricing and will not waste your time.

Which areas of the UK do you cover?

We recruit nationwide and can discuss engineering requirements across London, the Midlands, North West, Yorkshire, North East, South East, South West, Wales and Scotland. Candidate availability varies by discipline and working pattern.

Can you recruit engineers with specialist systems experience?

Yes. We can build searches around particular machinery, controls, automation platforms, manufacturing processes or project experience. Tell us which knowledge is essential and what can be learned after joining.
