FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Straight answers about Admin and HR recruitment with Clayton Miles.

Which Admin and HR roles can you recruit?

We recruit across general administration, office support, medical administration and human resources, from receptionists and administrators through to clinical coders, office managers and HR business partners.

Can you recruit medical and GP administration staff?

Yes. We can search for GP receptionists, medical administrators, medical secretaries, clinical coders, summarisers, care navigators and other patient-services professionals.

Do you support permanent and temporary recruitment?

Yes. We can discuss both permanent appointments and temporary staffing requirements, depending on the role and candidate availability.

How quickly can you send candidate CVs?

For many roles, suitable CVs can be available within 24–48 hours. Timing depends on location, requirements, working pattern and candidate availability.

How do you screen candidates?

We assess relevant experience, communication, motivation, availability and alignment with your brief before presenting a candidate.
