Our logistics hiring process

From the first conversation through to the candidate’s start date, every stage is handled by a consultant who understands your vacancy and the practical demands of your logistics operation.

  1. 1

    Initial consultation

    Your consultant will arrange a conversation at a time that works for you. Where practical, we can visit your depot, warehouse or office to understand the operation first-hand. We will discuss the team structure, shift pattern, location, pay, systems and the day-to-day demands of the vacancy, along with any licences, qualifications or sector experience the successful candidate must have.

  2. 2

    Shortlisting candidates

    We search our logistics and transport network and use targeted recruitment channels to find people with the right background. That may include experienced drivers, warehouse professionals, transport planners, coordinators or operational managers who are not actively applying through general job boards. We work quickly, but we do not send CVs simply to fill a shortlist. Every introduction must make sense for your vacancy.

  3. 3

    Interviewing candidates

    Before a candidate meets you, we speak with them in detail about their experience, availability and reasons for moving. We assess their suitability against the brief and personally explain the role, working environment and standards you expect. Checks are adapted to the position and can cover right-to-work evidence, driving licences, Driver CPC, tachograph cards, FLT training and relevant WMS or TMS experience.

  4. 4

    Securing a candidate

    Once you have chosen the right person, we manage communication, support the offer and keep in touch through to their agreed start date. If the first shortlist does not produce the right match, we return to the market and continue the search. The aim is not simply to fill the vacancy, but to secure someone who fits the role, the team and the realities of your logistics operation.

The hiring problem

Every workforce gap can disrupt the flow of goods.

Logistics recruitment spans safety-critical driving, fast-moving warehouse operations and commercially important planning. The right person must fit the role, shift, systems and operating environment.

Demand changes faster than headcount

Seasonal peaks, contract wins, absence and changing volumes can create gaps across transport and warehouse teams. Unfilled shifts risk delayed dispatches, missed delivery windows and pressure on the existing workforce.

Similar titles can hide different requirements

A transport planner handling trunking networks needs different experience from a last-mile coordinator. Warehouse roles vary by product, equipment, temperature, automation, shift pattern and expected throughput.

Poor fit affects safety and retention

Nights, rotating shifts, manual handling, productivity targets and customer contact all affect suitability. If practical expectations are missed, employers lose time to dropouts, unsuitable interviews and repeat hiring.

Logistics recruitment FAQ

What logistics hiring managers ask us.

Straight answers about roles, screening, candidate availability, compliance, turnaround and recruitment fees.

Which logistics and transport roles can Clayton Miles recruit?

We recruit HGV and delivery drivers, warehouse operatives, pickers and packers, forklift drivers, transport planners, logistics coordinators, warehouse supervisors, fleet managers and operational leaders.

How are logistics and transport candidates screened?

Screening is matched to the role. It can include identity and right-to-work evidence, relevant operational experience, systems knowledge, qualifications, references, availability and working preferences. Driver searches can also cover licence entitlement, endorsements, Driver CPC and tachograph-card evidence where required.

Can you help with urgent or short-notice cover?

Yes. We can support urgent transport and warehouse requirements where suitable candidates are available. Turnaround depends on the site or depot, shift, rate, role and any specialist credentials required.

Do you recruit permanent and temporary logistics staff?

Yes. We support permanent hires, planned temporary cover and urgent workforce requirements. We confirm the most suitable service after discussing the duration, volume and working pattern you need.

Can you recruit HGV drivers and check Driver CPC?

Yes. For professional driving assignments we can include the correct vocational licence, Driver CPC, endorsements and digital tachograph card within the agreed screening brief. Employers remain responsible for confirming legal and operator-specific requirements.

Can you recruit forklift and specialist warehouse staff?

Yes. We can search for forklift drivers and warehouse professionals with relevant equipment, goods-in, picking, inventory, dispatch or team-leadership experience. Tell us the truck type, training evidence, site and recency requirements.

How quickly can you provide candidates?

We aim to turn around suitable candidates within 24–48 hours for many requirements. Specialist positions, difficult shifts, high-volume bookings and some locations may take longer, and we set realistic expectations once the brief is clear.

How much does logistics recruitment cost?

Permanent recruitment is offered on a contingency basis with no upfront search fee. Temporary and contract rates depend on the role, shift, location, notice period and assignment requirements. We confirm the applicable terms before work begins.

Which areas of the UK do you cover?

We recruit nationwide and can discuss logistics and transport requirements across London, the Midlands, North West, Yorkshire, North East, South East, South West, Wales and Scotland. Candidate availability varies by site and shift pattern.
